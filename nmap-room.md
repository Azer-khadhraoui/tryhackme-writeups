## Key Concepts
- **Ports** direct traffic to the right application on a server.
- **65535** ports available per computer.
- Ports **0–1023** = "well-known ports" (**1024** total) — e.g. `22` SSH, `53` DNS, `80` HTTP, `443` HTTPS.
- Port states: **open**, **closed**, **filtered**.
- **Nmap** = industry-standard tool for port scanning & enumeration.
